# Marketing and adoption

The mentors listed "Marketing" as a point to address. We read it as three things, with the emphasis on **passenger adoption and convenience** and **pitch positioning** (confirmed by the team). If the mentors meant something narrower, update this file.

## 1. Passenger adoption and convenience (main focus)

Passengers adopt Flare only if scanning is easier than walking to a desk, and if they trust the code.

- **Placement:** put codes where problems are noticed and people wait: near lifts, toilets, seating areas, information points. Exact locations are an assumption. We do not claim anything about the real Craiova layout.
- **Signage:** a clear Romanian and English sign next to every code that says what it does, that it is not an emergency line, and that the page never asks for payment, passwords or personal data. This also supports the QR security story (see [security and abuse](security-and-abuse.md)).
- **Convenience:** no app, no account, about 30 seconds, large buttons, works one-handed and on a slow connection.
- **Trust:** the short URL printed beneath the code, honest AI labelling, a clear promise of what happens next.

Draft signage text (to review with a native speaker before any use):

- RO: "Ai observat o problemă? Scanează și spune-ne. Nu cerem niciodată plăți, parole sau date personale. Nu este o linie de urgență: pentru urgențe sună la 112."
- EN: "Noticed a problem? Scan and tell us. This page never asks for payment, passwords or personal data. This is not an emergency line: in an emergency call 112."

## 2. Airport buy-in

- **Cheap pilot:** a few printed codes and a web page, no hardware.
- **What the airport gets:** one place to see issues, duplicates merged into prioritised issues, and fewer missed problems.
- Costs, ownership and who maintains the codes are unknown. Mark them as assumptions and do not quote numbers.

## 3. Pitch positioning

- Designed for non-technical staff and stressed passengers.
- Honest about QR impersonation: signage plus detection, not a false promise.
- Spam and quality are handled: rate limits, quarantine, and a quality gate that tells passengers what to fix.
- Transparent to passengers (AI labelled, no pretending to be human).
- Duplicate grouping turns noise into priority.
- Romanian-first.
- **Do not claim novelty.** Competing products exist (for example AeroSimple, Sensfix, AirVote, Zello QR Assist, Fixalert). The list is unverified. Before any comparison in the pitch, verify their actual features from primary sources (a research task for later).
- Pitch entry-page description is drafted in [decisions](decisions.md) only as a reference; keep it consistent with this positioning.
