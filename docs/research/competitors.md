## What this means for the pitch

Flare is not a new idea. **Aerosimple's Vigil is the closest match:** a QR code to a mobile web form in airports, AI triage and routing to teams, clustering of repeated reports at one location, and emergency keywords that skip the queue. Sensfix describes a similar photo-based flow. Flare's emphasis is different:
- staff and Passengers with no training
- **Romanian first** (none of the sources we checked mentions Romanian)
- an open, explained stance on fake QR codes and spam (we found no public description of such measures in these sources)
- Questions answered instantly from a Knowledge base instead of reaching staff

**In the pitch:** never say "first" or "only". Say "existing products like Aerosimple and Sensfix show the need; Flare focuses on…". Present any comparison as "according to their public pages, checked 31 October 2026".
