# Ideas and stretch goals

Parking lot. Nothing here is committed. Items move into [product](product.md) only when the team decides.

## Stretch goals already named

- Photo upload with validation, a size limit and metadata stripping.
- An admin QR code inventory, anomaly alerts for odd scan patterns, a routine physical check of QR codes.
- A management view of trends, hotspots and response times.
- A shift handover view and notes on Issues.
- Airline and handling-agent Teams for Issues they own.
- Staff splitting and merging Issues manually.
- Push notifications to the Staff app for Critical Issues.

## Ideas raised in discussion

- **Quick-start suggestions** under the text field, such as "Something is broken", "Cleaning needed" or "I need help". Tapping one fills in the start of a sentence, which helps Passengers who find typing hard.
- **"Send anyway for staff review"** after repeated Bounces. See [AI pipeline](triage-and-dedup.md).
- **An anonymised Ticket code or boarding-pass scan** for lost luggage. Under review and probably dropped: lost luggage is handled by the airline's baggage desk, and the proposal is to answer it from the Knowledge base instead. See [product](product.md).

## Out of scope (not ideas)

A native passenger app, real airport or airline integration, payments, real emergency dispatch, real deployment, flight data, Passenger accounts, two-way chat.
