# Ideas and stretch goals

Parking lot. Nothing here is committed. Items move into [product](product.md) only when the team decides.

## Stretch goals already named

- Photo upload with validation, size limit and metadata stripping.
- Admin QR inventory, anomaly alerts for odd scan patterns, a routine physical check of codes.
- Management view: trends, hotspots, response times.
- Shift handover view, notes on issues.
- Staff roles, teams, team filters, role-based default views and routing.
- Airline and handling-agent staff for issues they own.
- A passenger status page (private link showing received, seen, resolved).
- Staff splitting and merging issues manually.

## Ideas raised in discussion

- Ticket or boarding pass scan for lost luggage and similar, with a minimal-data fallback if there is no ticket. See [product](product.md).
- "Send anyway for human review" after repeated quality-gate rejections. See [triage and dedup](triage-and-dedup.md).

## Out of scope (not ideas)

Native app, real airport integration, payments, real emergency dispatch, real deployment, flight data, passenger accounts, two-way chat.
